# Design System: Nuova Perú · Landing corporativa

> **Status: final world pending the client's choice.** The client commissioned three alternative directions of the same B2B landing (A "Despacho", B "Cenefa", C "Plano"). All three are built, have passed finish review, and are recorded here from their shipped CSS. **When the client picks one:** delete the other two `## Propuesta …` sections, remove their prefixed tokens (`a-*`, `b-*`, `c-*`) from the frontmatter and from `.impeccable/design.json`, then promote the chosen world's subsections into the canonical Colors / Typography / Layout / Elevation & Depth / Shapes / Components / Do's and Don'ts headings and drop the letter prefix. Until then, a new surface must declare which world it belongs to. Never mix tokens from two worlds on one page.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Universo de Góndolas" (shared brand layer; each world has its own North Star below)**

Nuova sells corporate store roll-outs to large retail chains. The visual system is split into one durable brand layer and three candidate worlds. The brand layer holds what does not change whichever world wins: the binding yellow and near-black pair, the logo files, the Spanish (Perú) voice, the shared lead form and its WhatsApp hand-off, the proof and truth rules, and the WhatsApp floating button. Each world reads the same content through one physical metaphor taken from Nuova's own trade: the shipment leaving the Ate plant (A), the gondola shelf with its yellow cenefa and hanging hablador (B), and the store floor plan (C).

Every world is static HTML/CSS/JS with one inline `<style>` block, self-hosted fonts (`assets/fonts/fonts.css`) and the shared `assets/js/lead.js`. Every world honours `prefers-reduced-motion` by cancelling all animation and transition, and shares the house ease `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One binding colour pair, #FFD207 + #1E1E1C, used at full strength in every world; yellow is a material (tape, shelf edge, built fixture), never a decorative tint.
- Heavy, tight display type (900 weight, line-height 0.8 to 0.98) over a plain, readable body face.
- Square-cut forms: radii of 0 to 3px everywhere except the round WhatsApp button and status dot.
- Numbers presented as manifest or sheet lines with tabular figures.
- Only verifiable nuova.pe facts as proof; anything else is marked as a placeholder.

## Colors

The shared layer owns two colours. Everything else belongs to a world.

### Primary
- **Nuova Yellow** (brand-yellow): binding corporate primary. Packing tape in A, cenefa and hablador in B, built fixtures and the primary button in C. Always paired with Nuova Ink for text on it.

### Neutral
- **Nuova Ink** (brand-ink): binding corporate secondary. Text and rules in A and B, the drafting ground in C, footer bands in A and B, button fill in A and B.

### Named Rules
**The Binding Pair Rule.** #FFD207 and #1E1E1C appear in every world, unmodified. Text on yellow is always ink. No world swaps either colour for a nearby hue; B's darker yellow lip (b-yellow-lip) only shades the edge of a yellow object and is never a flat fill on its own.

## Typography

All faces are self-hosted from `assets/fonts/` via `assets/fonts/fonts.css` (Big Shoulders Stencil Display, Archivo, Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Chivo, Chivo Mono, latin + latin-ext). No world loads a font from a CDN. Each world uses its own pairing, recorded in its section.

## Components

### Lead form (shared behaviour, `assets/js/lead.js`)
- **Contract:** any `form[data-lead]`. Fields are wrapped in `[data-campo]` with a sibling `[data-error]` live region; `select[data-departamentos]` is filled with the 24 departments plus Callao, Lima preselected.
- **Validation:** on blur (when filled) and live once a field is invalid; sets `aria-invalid`. Peruvian mobile `9XXXXXXXX` (optional +51), email pattern, store count ≥ 1. Error copy is specific and in Spanish ("Escribe un teléfono de 9 dígitos (ej. 987 654 321).").
- **Submit:** no endpoint yet. Builds a message that starts "Hola Nuova, quiero una propuesta de implementación corporativa." and lists each field, then opens `wa.me/51903260296` in a new tab. The button reads "Abriendo WhatsApp…" for 2.5s, and the `[data-ok]` confirmation is revealed and focused. When an endpoint exists, only this file changes.
- **Online state:** `[data-estado]` shows "Asesor en línea · lun–sáb 8:00–18:00" or "Fuera de horario · te respondemos desde las 8:00", computed from Lima time (UTC-5). `body.is-off` greys the status dot in every world.

### WhatsApp FAB (shared)
- **Shape:** fixed 58px circle, 18px from the bottom-right corner, white WhatsApp glyph drawn as inline SVG at 30px, soft dark drop shadow, scale(1.06) on hover. The green is set per world (a-whatsapp, b-whatsapp, c-whatsapp), each tuned to its ground.

### Logo
- `nuova-logo-black.png` on light grounds (A, B). `assets/img/logo-nuova-light-1.png` on the dark ground (C, picker page). Header height 44 to 46px desktop, 36 to 38px mobile. Favicon: `assets/img/cropped-icono-nuova-2-192x192.png`.

### Client logos
- Twelve real Nuova client logos from `assets/clientes/`, shown monochrome and labelled "Algunas de las empresas que ya trabajan con Nuova." Each world sets its own monochrome treatment.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** write in Spanish (Perú), professional and close, speaking to chains and roll-outs rather than single shops.
- **Do** limit proof to nuova.pe facts: "+50 empresas", "cientos de negocios", 24 departamentos, Google reviews 5★, and the product counts from the nuova.pe catalogue.
- **Do** caption imagery as what it is: 3D renders as "Diseño 3D · …", the floor plan as "Plano ilustrativo · medidas referenciales", missing project photos as a visibly marked placeholder ("Foto de proyecto pendiente").
- **Do** route every lead form through `form[data-lead]` and `assets/js/lead.js`, keeping WhatsApp +51 903 260 296 visible next to the form and as the fixed FAB.
- **Do** draw every icon, pictogram and glyph as inline SVG.

### Don't:
- **Don't** show Sodimac, Promart, Plaza Vea, Tottus or Oechsle as clients; they are prospects.
- **Don't** invent years in business, plant size, capacity, certifications, named testimonials, delivery times or product dimensions.
- **Don't** mix tokens or signature devices from two proposals on one surface.

## Propuesta A: Despacho

**Creative North Star: "The Dispatch"**: every store opening is a shipment leaving Nuova's Ate plant, labelled for its destination.

### Colors
A warm corrugated-board world: kraft ground, black stencil ink, yellow packing tape.
- **Kraft Board** (a-kraft): page ground, with a fractal-noise SVG fibre texture layered on top. **Kraft Shadow** (a-kraft-lo): scrollbar track.
- **Label Stock** (a-label): shipping labels, the route sheet, the crate panel, photo prints.
- **Stencil Ink** (brand-ink) for type and rules; **Burnt Ink** (a-ink-brown) for secondary paragraphs; **Field Label Brown** (a-field-label) for the 11px field captions.
- **Tape Yellow** (brand-yellow): the tape strips, the h1 highlight, the label's tape strap, hover and active fills on rows and routes, the confirmation strip.
- **Focus Wash** (a-focus-wash): the background of a focused label field and a hovered route-sheet row.
- **Stamp Red** (a-error): field errors.
- **Night band** (`.routes` section and footer): ink ground, a-night-text body, a-night-mute small print, a-night-rule grid lines.

**The Tape Owns The Seams Rule.** Yellow appears as full-width packing tape across section seams (top of page, after the hero, before the close) and as the strap holding each label. It is a material, not a scattered accent.

### Typography
**Display Font:** Big Shoulders Stencil Display 900 (fallback Arial Narrow). **Body Font:** Archivo 400 to 800.
- **Display** (a-display): hero and closing headlines, uppercase, max 11 to 13ch, `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Headline** (a-headline): section heads, uppercase, max 16ch.
- **Title** (a-title): route-sheet steps, crate and pictogram titles; product list items run at clamp(30px,3.6vw,46px); the route-sheet step numbers at 64px.
- **Body / Lead** (a-body, a-lead): Archivo, 44 to 46ch measure, Burnt Ink.
- **Label** (a-label): field and panel captions, 11 to 13px, 700 to 800, uppercase, 0.06 to 0.14em tracking, tabular figures for codes.

**The Stencil Is Cargo Rule.** Stencil type is for things painted on the box: headlines, numbers, destination codes, list items. Running text and form input stay in Archivo.

### Layout
Centred container (a-max) with fluid gutters (a-gutter). The hero is a 1.25fr / 1fr split: headline and a 3-line manifest on the left, the shipping-label form on the right. Sections use a-section vertical padding, with a two-column intro (headline / paragraph aligned at the bottom). Photos sit in a 1.4fr/1fr/1fr strip. The destination board is a 5-column grid (4 at ≤1080px, 2 at ≤760px). Breakpoints: 1080px (hero stacks, label straightens), 760px (nav hidden, intros single column), 440px (label fields single column, barcode hidden).

### Elevation & Depth
Hybrid: paper objects lying on cardboard. Depth comes from warm brown, negative-spread contact shadows plus small rotations, never from a cool grey glow.
- **Tape** (`0 2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.06), 0 6px 14px -8px rgba(60,35,10,.55)`), with a white-to-transparent top sheen.
- **Label** (`0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.4) inset, 0 22px 40px -22px rgba(60,35,10,.75), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(60,35,10,.35)`).
- **Print** (`0 18px 30px -20px rgba(60,35,10,.8)`), **Crate** (`0 26px 40px -26px rgba(60,35,10,.9)`).
- **Button lift** (`0 8px 16px -8px rgba(30,30,28,.7)`) on hover only.

**The Paper-On-Board Rule.** Loose paper (labels, prints) may rotate up to ±0.8deg; rotation is removed at ≤1080px. Shadows stay warm brown (rgba(60,35,10,…)).

### Shapes
Ruled rectangles. Panels have a 3px ink frame and 1px internal rules; manifest lines are 1px dashed. Radii: 2px on buttons, 0 on labels and fields. The tape has torn ends (an 8×12px zig-zag mask) and repeats its phrase in whole units only, refitted on resize.

### Components
- **Buttons:** Archivo 800 uppercase at 90% width. Primary is ink with yellow text (a-button-primary); ghost is outlined in ink and fills on hover; the tape variant is yellow with ink text. Hover lifts 2px with the button shadow; active resets; disabled is 0.6 opacity with a progress cursor.
- **Shipping label (lead form):** header row with stencil title and a destination-code cell ("LIM"), a dashed route line "Ate, Lima → destino", a two-column ruled field grid, and a footer with a generated barcode ("NUO-LIM-0001") beside a full-height submit "Despachar solicitud". Fields have no box, a 2px ink underline on focus, Focus Wash on the cell, and a Stamp Red underline when invalid.
- **Route sheet (process):** a 4-row table framed in 3px ink: number / title / text / tag. On hover the row takes Focus Wash and its number cell turns yellow.
- **Product list + crate:** a stencil tab list (hover or selected: indents 18px and fills yellow) driving a sticky crate panel whose image cross-fades (opacity plus scale .98).
- **Destination board:** an ink band holding a grid of department buttons with ISO codes; hover, focus and pressed turn the cell yellow.
- **Handling marks:** ISO 780-style pictograms drawn in SVG inside 92px ink-framed squares.
- **Client logos:** grayscale(1) contrast(1.8) with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, printed onto the kraft.

**Signature interaction.** Choosing a destination (in the label select or on the board) re-routes the label: the destination code, the route line, the barcode and its number update, and the board marks the matching cell pressed. On load, the top tape pulls across once (`clip-path` inset, 1.1s, house ease).

### Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** number figures and steps like manifest lines (01, 02, N° 003) in tabular stencil figures.
- **Do** keep forms and data panels as ruled shipping labels on Label Stock.
- **Don't** break the tape mid-phrase; it repeats only in whole units.
- **Don't** use cool grey shadows or radii above 2px.

## Propuesta B: Cenefa

**Creative North Star: "The Gondola"**: content sits on shelves, and every claim is a shelf-edge hablador.

### Colors
White powder-coated steel, yellow shelf edges, black price tags.
- **Powder Steel** (b-steel): page ground. **Steel Shadow** (b-steel-2): header rule, scrollbar track.
- **Upright Grey** (b-upright) with **Upright Edge** (b-upright-edge): the perforated uprights.
- **Shelf White** (b-shelf-white): shelf interiors, header, inputs, product tags, the closing card.
- **Cenefa Yellow** (brand-yellow) with **Cenefa Lip** (b-yellow-lip): yellow shelf bands, habladores and the highlight button; the lip is a 2 to 3px shaded edge and the star fill.
- **Tag Ink** (brand-ink): ink habladores, the selected product tag, the night band (`.why`) and the footer. **Graphite** (b-ink-2): secondary text. **Night Text** (b-night-text): footer copy.
- **Error Red** (b-error): field errors.

**The Cenefa Is The Shelf Rule.** Yellow bands with a darker lip mark where content rests: the top of the hero bay, the form bar, under section heads (12px), the process rail (22px), the gallery base (16px). Yellow is a shelf or a tag, not a highlight.

### Typography
**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed 600 to 900 (fallback Arial Narrow). **Body Font:** Barlow 400 to 600.
- **Display** (b-display): the hero headline, set on a hablador, uppercase, max 12ch.
- **Headline** (b-headline): section heads, uppercase, max 15ch.
- **Numeral** (b-numeral): the big price-tag figures ("24", "+50"), tabular.
- **Title** (b-title): step and tag titles at 22 to 28px.
- **Body / Lede** (b-body, b-lede): Barlow, 42 to 48ch measure.
- **Label** (b-label): field labels, chips, captions; Barlow Condensed 600 to 800, uppercase, 0.04 to 0.1em tracking. The nav also uses Barlow Condensed 600 at 18px, uppercase.

**The Price Tag Rule.** Claims are numerals or short words in heavy condensed type sitting on a tag, with a 2px rule separating them from the explanation.

### Layout
Container b-max with gutter b-gutter. The hero is a gondola bay: two perforated uprights (b-post wide) frame a full-width real gondola photo with the hablador headline hanging from the top cenefa, and the quick form runs along the bottom cenefa as a 6-column bar. The product shelf is a 1.05fr/1fr display above a 6-column tag rail. The process is 4 hablador steps hung from a rail; the "why" section is 3 tags on a dark rack. The gallery alternates uprights and photos in one grid. Breakpoints: 1180px (form 3 columns, rail 3, steps 2), 820px (nav hidden, single columns, uprights narrow to 12px), 560px (form 2 columns, rail 2, shelf uprights hidden).

### Elevation & Depth
Lifted: objects hang from or sit on steel.
- **Cenefa** (`0 -3px 0 #e9bd00 inset, 0 10px 18px -12px rgba(30,30,28,.5)`) plus a 5px white sheen on top.
- **Hablador** (`0 18px 28px -16px rgba(30,30,28,.55), 0 2px 0 #e9bd00`); the ink variant uses a `#000` lip, the white variant a `#c9c9c2` lip.
- **Product tag** (`0 2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.08), 0 10px 16px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.5)`).
- **Upright** (`inset 2px 0 0 #bdbeb8, inset -2px 0 0 #bdbeb8`) carrying the slot pattern.
- **Button lift** (`0 10px 18px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.7)`) on hover only.

**The Whole Slot Rule.** The perforated-upright pattern repeats in whole 25px units (`background-repeat: round`); a slot is never cut in half.

### Shapes
Tags and inputs are near-square (2 to 3px). Every tag carries a dark clip on top (34×24px on habladores, 24×16px on product tags) and swings from it (`transform-origin` above the tag). Habladores rest at small set rotations (−1.6 to +1.6deg) that reset to 0 on mobile.

### Components

## Propuesta C: Plano

**Creative North Star: "The Floor Plan"**: Nuova draws, builds and installs every fixture on the plan.

### Colors
A near-black drafting sheet with warm grey linework, where only built fixtures are yellow.
- **Drafting Ground** (brand-ink): page ground. **Sheet** (c-ground-2): drawing sheets and the photo frame.
- **Linework** (c-line) for rules and borders; **Linework High** (c-line-hi) for dimension lines, unbuilt fixtures and the line button.
- **Drafting Paper** (c-paper): primary text, walls, title-block rules, the tooltip.
- **Pencil Grey** (c-mute): secondary text and labels.
- **Fixture Yellow** (brand-yellow): built fixtures, the primary button, the active phase, emphasis words in headlines, codes in the title block, focus outlines.
- **Cell Focus** (c-focus-wash): focused title-block cells and hovered legend rows.
- **Error Coral** (c-error): field errors, tuned for the dark ground. **Online Green** (c-online): the status dot.

**The Yellow Is Installed Rule.** On the plan, yellow means built. Unbuilt fixtures are dashed Linework High; yellow fill arrives with the installation phase. Off the plan, yellow marks only the one primary action, the active state and a single emphasis word.

### Typography

**The Mono For Measures Rule.** Chivo Mono is used only for codes, dimensions, sheet numbers and figures. It never sets prose or headings.

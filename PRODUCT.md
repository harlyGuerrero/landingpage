## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). Must be easy to upload to any hosting or embed in WordPress. No build step.

## Users

Decision makers at large Peruvian retail chains — home improvement (Sodimac Perú, Promart), supermarkets/hypermarkets (Plaza Vea, Tottus), department stores (Oechsle) and similar. Roles: store expansion / new store openings, visual merchandising, procurement (compras), operations and store-format managers. They evaluate suppliers that can equip many stores on schedule, to spec, nationwide. These chains are prospects, not current clients.

## Product Purpose

Nuova Perú ("Universo de Góndolas", nuova.pe) designs, manufactures and installs commercial store fixtures. This landing page offers corporate-scale implementations: equipping new stores, remodels and roll-outs for large retail chains. Success = a qualified corporate lead (company, role, scope of project) via form, or a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

Integrated in-house chain: 3D design → own fabrication → professional installation → nationwide delivery → ongoing support, from a single Peruvian manufacturer in Ate, Lima. Made-to-measure rather than catalog import.

## Operating Context

Corporate buyers compare suppliers on capacity, deadlines, consistency across stores, coverage outside Lima, customization to their store format, and after-sales support. Typical next step is a technical meeting or request for proposal.

## Product Principles

1. Speak to scale: multi-store roll-outs, consistency and deadlines, not single-shop sales.
2. One accountable supplier from design to installation.
3. Proof over adjectives; only verifiable claims.
4. Make the corporate next step low-friction: a short qualified form, WhatsApp always at hand.
